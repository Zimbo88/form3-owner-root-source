## Review and provenance

The source edition initially selected only `img_4040.jpg`. The first review on
this date restored the previously reviewed `img_4039.jpg` and `img_4044.jpg`.
The wider views had been held back because of potentially individual component
labels. Following my explicit request to include the remaining photographs,
`img_4042.jpg`, `img_4045.jpg` and `img_4048.jpg` received a fresh visual review and
were added unchanged. The earlier exclusion is historical, not the current policy.

Visible board/component markings, small two-dimensional component codes and
commercial packaging remain intentionally visible. No readable access credential,
personal document or account screen was observed. The codes were not decoded;
this review does not claim the photographs are anonymous or free of identifiers.
Inclusion reflects permission to use my photographs, not a general legal clearance.

All six files match the retained deployment-kit hashes, decode successfully as
JPEG and have zero EXIF entries. Absence of metadata alone does not establish
that visible labels are suitable for every reuse. Five images are 1200 × 1600;
`img_4040.jpg` is 334 × 518. Exact hashes are in the reviewed allowlist and generated
source checksums. No original was overwritten, cropped or retouched.

Photo credit: **Mathias Zimmermann**. These are historical repair observations,
not new hardware acceptance or compatibility evidence. Photographs do not replace
the guide's electrical identification, measurement, readback or reassembly checks.
The unrelated PNG in `docs/assets/print-diagnostics-DEMO.png` is a labeled
synthetic panel screenshot, not one of the six owner hardware photographs.
